**in summary**

i came into this course  excited about social marketing and its ability to move a finding into the world rather than simply document it. most of my training has been on the nitty gritty research side. i believe in the importance of that work, but i have watched good evidence sit unused, and this course presented a structured way to think about how to get individuals to act on science.  this course showed me that a social marketing campaign has the same structure as the science i'm fond of. watching the full arc take shape across the designs from strategy through implementation made it click.

running therapeutic groups and conducted assessments in residential addiction treatment is where the impetus for meeting people where they are resonates strongly with me. information rarely fails for lack of clarity, but it often does due to poor fit of the constraints and fears of a real person.

this is why i want to continue my work in the field of health education. the gap between what science knows and what people actually do is enormous. to close it we need science fused with social marketing that is rigorouss and empathetic. this course helped me grow further competence in a way that my research experience could not. 

moving forward, what excites me most is getting evidence-based ideas adopted with ferocity. whether it's in a doctoral program furthering the domain of weight loss research or working in the quality department at a hospital, the tools i've gained from this education will serve me well.

thank you,

joshua
