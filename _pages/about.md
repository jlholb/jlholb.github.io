i am a data analyst at the george washington university transplant institute and a master's student in health education & behavior at the university of florida, where i expect to complete my degree in december 2026. i am currently applying to doctoral programs with the aim of conducting research on weight loss and physical activity interventions and how we can improve their adherence and effectiveness.

**research interests**

my work sits at the intersection of health behavior change, obesity treatment, and the measurement problems that come with studying both.

as a research assistant in the mcvay weight management lab, i contributed to instrument development for a national survey of glp-1 users, building construct tables and synthesizing adverse event data from the step and surmount trials. rapid uptake of these medications has outpaced our understanding of how patients actually experience them outside of trial conditions, meriting robust research to find insights into the real behaviors surrounding these wonder drugs.

i produced literature syntheses supporting a federal grant proposal on ai-based behavior change interventions, including identifying validated measures of technology trust and attitudes toward ai. as these tools move into practice, whether patients trust them may matter as much as whether they work.

across the mcvay lab, mathematica, and the abcd study, much of my work has been the unglamorous infrastructure of good research: survey specifications, testing protocols, sample reconciliation, data cleaning, and quality assurance. findings are only as trustworthy as the instruments and procedures that produce them.

**background**

before graduate school i spent three years at mathematica policy research as a research associate, leading components of national evaluations including snacs and srae where I designed survey specifications, ran qualitative studies and usability tests, and managed data transfers across research. earlier, i coordinated a 450-participant site for the adolescent brain cognitive development study, and worked as a group facilitator and case manager in residential addiction treatment, where i ran therapeutic groups and conducted clinical assessments.
